# Authority Sources

Risk research uses a small trigger-based registry of authoritative sources.
These are seed locations, not evidence that any rule applies to a candidate.
Retrieve and version the current source only after a candidate-side trigger
justifies it.

## Review Priority

1. Official regulator.
2. Official platform policy.
3. Official marketplace guide.
4. Statute, court, or case record.
5. Qualified legal analysis.
6. Operator material.
7. Community discussion.

Priority does not establish applicability. Preserve conflicts, jurisdiction,
dates, evidence direction, and uncertainty.

## Conditional Government Interfaces

- Federal Register API: proposed/final rules, notices, agencies, dates, and CFR
  references after a domain trigger.
- openFDA: device classifications, clearances, labels, adverse events, or
  recalls after a medical-device trigger. Similar records do not classify a
  candidate.
- eCFR: current machine-readable regulations after a known family and citation
  exist; not broad semantic search.
- Regulations.gov: later docket and rulemaking enrichment.
- CFPB Consumer Complaint Database: later financial complaint enrichment,
  coordinated through shared evidence ownership.
