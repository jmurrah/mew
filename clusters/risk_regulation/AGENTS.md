# Risk Regulation Instructions

- Read `CONTEXT.md` completely before changing this cluster.
- Pair every risk claim with candidate-side trigger and authority-side evidence.
- Operate as an evidence layer, not legal adviser, compliance checker, or
  opportunity gatekeeper.
- Keep topic/function, policy/enforcement, requested/inferred data, and
  inherent/optional risk separate.
- Reserve hard-stop flags for explicit authoritative requirements and always
  route them to human review.
- Keep future logic in `risk_regulation.py` after shared interfaces exist.
